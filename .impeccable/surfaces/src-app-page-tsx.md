# Surface brief: marketing landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: a restaurant owner or manager in Pakistan, usually on a phone. Action: message DekhKhao on WhatsApp ("Talk to Us"). Proof on hand: the hero AR size demo (pizza scan), the AR demo recording (third-party ar-code.com example, labelled), the live-demo QR (ar-code.com hosted pizza), the scroll story, the size comparator, the live 3D viewer. Constraints: brand identity sheet pinned (2026-11-03); no invented customers, stats or prices; not a POS / ordering / digital menu; DekhKhao written as one word.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the brand sticker sheet come to life: food snaps onto the table at real size the way the cut-paper pieces of the Urdu mark snap together. It refuses the SaaS split hero with gradient wash and a row of equal feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cream ground; Dekh Teal owns whole fields (demo, technology, footer); Khao Orange, Zest Yellow and Pop Pink appear only as flat cut-paper shapes, a highlighter, hand-drawn swooshes and three-line sparks. Sora ExtraBold tight display, Caveat for handwritten asides. Teal pill buttons, line icons with an orange fill note, 16px radii, flat colour, almost no shadow.

STORY: The visitor learns DekhKhao shows food at its real size in AR (not a menu, not POS), sees it working (size toggle, demo video, a QR they can scan right now), learns the restaurant just sends dishes and DekhKhao runs the rest, then messages on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left half: the geometric Urdu mark beside a stacked "Dekh / Khao" wordmark, handwritten tagline with an orange swoosh, one sentence with yellow highlighter on "real size", Talk to Us pill plus Watch the demo play-circle. Right half: phone over a table on a cut-paper composition (teal hill, yellow wedge, pink dots, sparks) with the working 10/12/14-inch size toggle. Mobile stacks the same order.

FORM: Brand-pinned world from the supplied identity sheet; no roll. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
